Built with women,
for digital control.

CYBERAURA exists because the online world was not designed with women's safety in mind. We turn confusing, frightening digital threats into clear understanding, organised evidence and a calm plan of action — without ever taking ownership of a woman's identity or data away from her.

Our mission

To give every woman and girl the intelligence, tools and confidence to understand digital threats, protect her privacy and respond to incidents with dignity — on her own terms.

{{ ui.icon('privacy') }}

Our promise

Privacy is the product, not a policy page. Evidence stays in your browser, narratives are never stored, and every prototype capability is labelled honestly. You remain in control.

{{ ui.icon('shield-heart') }}

Our approach

Not a generic safety website. A modern threat-intelligence platform: classify, preserve, assess, respond and connect to verified official support — a complete journey.

Empowerment is the ability
to stay online safely.

Forcing women offline is not protection — it is exclusion. CYBERAURA supports digital inclusion by helping women remain present in education, work and public life with the knowledge and tools to defend their space. Safety that preserves participation, not retreat.

{{ ui.icon('users', 'icon icon-xs') }} Co-design with the community

Women shape every feature

CYBERAURA is built with the women it serves, not just for them. Participation is concrete and ongoing:

  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Feedback loopsEvery analysis and checklist ends with a chance to tell us what was confusing, missing or wrong — directly below.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Usability testingWomen and girls trial each flow with fictional scenarios, so language, steps and visuals are tested by the people who will rely on them.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Community consultationSurvivors, students, educators and advocates review the playbooks and resource directory before they ship, keeping guidance grounded in lived reality.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Accessible by defaultPlain language, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support and mobile-first layouts are requirements, not afterthoughts.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    A standing community hubThe Women's Safety Feedback & Resource Hub collects anonymous challenge notes and a supported suggestions board, so lived experience keeps steering what gets built.

{{ ui.icon('lock', 'icon icon-sm') }} Privacy commitments

  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Local-first evidenceFiles and hashes live in your browser's IndexedDB. They are never uploaded to a server or an AI service.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Minimal collectionIncident narratives are processed in memory and never written to disk. Only non-sensitive metadata is stored.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    No surveillanceWe never monitor your devices, accounts or social media, and never run unauthorized scans.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Your data, your choiceClear your workspace at any time from the dashboard. Quick exit leaves the site instantly.
  • {{ ui.icon('check', 'icon icon-xs') }}
    Honest labellingSimulated analyses, sample statistics and prototype limits are always stated plainly.
